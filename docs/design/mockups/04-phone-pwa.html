What this mock settles, and what to check

  • One Router transcript. The phone shows the same single conversation as the desktop. Tasks and their helpers appear only in the Tasks drawer, never as chat threads.
  • Five sections, in order: Needs you, Working, Completed, Pinned, and a collapsed Archive that shows a count. A pinned task lives only in Pinned and keeps its phase text. Helpers nest under their parent and are not top-level rows.
  • Stop all running threads stays reachable. Every frame with a running task keeps the quiet running bar, including under the Tasks drawer (frame 3), the inspector sheet (frame 4), and the approval (frame 5).
  • Approve appears only for CapabilityApproval. Login, MFA, captcha, consent with a legal effect, and rotation show “Finish this on the desktop.” instead; the Task 5 mock shows those banners. The approval sheet carries the same content as the desktop approval, and Stop task is available there.
  • The composer stays enabled while a task waits. Frame 5 keeps it live. Sending a message does not approve, deny, or waive anything; those are host decisions made on the approval or the waiver banner.
  • Touch targets are 44 px or larger. Every button, chip, and tab in the five frames is measured in the browser.
  • Install banner. Shown in the browser only. In standalone mode it is hidden and Settings shows “Installed”. Not now hides the banner; Settings still offers Install.
  • Drawer and sheets. Frames 3 and 4 show open states only. The focus trap and focus restore to the trigger are specified in the accessibility section and are not simulated here.
  • Full-screen preview. Quote, Share, History, and More mirror the desktop overlay (Task 3). Quote keeps the source's taint label.
